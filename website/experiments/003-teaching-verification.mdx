## Numerical checks

`scripts/verify_lesson_math.py` imported the actual pinned verl advantage and loss functions in the existing vp environment. [Results](/assets/source/experiments/results/003-lesson-math.json.txt) confirm:

- Mean reward 0.125, sample standard deviation 0.341565, advantages -0.365961/+2.561730 for fourteen zeros and two ones.
- Near-zero loss and equivalent-logit derivatives approximately [+0.160108,-0.160108].
- Zero derivatives when old/current log-probabilities incorrectly share the graph.
- Five clipping/dual-clip examples, masked tool positions, and uniform-outcome groups.
- Correct versus mixed prompt grouping, temperature, binomial probability, gradient scaling, and query value calculations.

Equivalent logits were reconstructed from recorded probabilities, not extracted raw Qwen logits. Their derivatives are not full-model gradient norms.
