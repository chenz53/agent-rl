## Learn the mechanism before adopting a recipe

Each chapter starts with a concrete question, derives its assumptions, executes a small example, and examines failure modes. PyTorch is the teaching framework. Existing public infrastructure handles the production path; small kernels make its statistical choices inspectable.

The first edition includes fourteen new chapters with fifteen executable blocks and seven existing oncology lesson files. Outputs are captured from actual executions. The frontier review is dated **2026-10-13** and distinguishes mathematical results, public implementation evidence, and recent research proposals.

Read [the research alternatives](/tutorial/chapters/11-research-frontier) and [the source ledger](/tutorial/sources) before treating a technique as a default. This edition is an executable foundation with a bounded categorical-policy capstone; autonomous clinical model training remains a separate milestone.
